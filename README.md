# Ethics of AI in Education

A public writing portfolio connected to my **Ethics of Artificial Intelligence** studies at the University of Leeds.

I examined bias, accountability, cognitive debt and the need to align educational AI with sound pedagogy and human agency. The work brings ethical analysis into decisions about the design, evaluation and governance of educational technology.

## Published writing

### Pedagogy, cognitive debt and agency

[The Cognitive Debt Crisis: Designing Ethical AI for Deeper Learning](https://saqibsafdar.com/the-cognitive-debt-crisis-designing-ethical-ai-for-deeper-learning/)

An essay arguing that educational AI should support learners' thinking, productive effort and independence. It connects pedagogical design with institutional responsibility and governance.

### Bias and accountability in educational decisions

[How an Algorithm Deepened Inequality in UK A-Level Results—and What It Teaches Us About AI Fairness](https://saqibsafdar.com/bias-in-the-2020-a-level-grading-algorithm/)

A case-based ethical discussion of fairness, group-level assumptions and accountability in consequential educational decisions.

### Algorithmic bias and public institutions

[The Mathematics of Injustice: Why Facial Recognition’s Algorithmic Bias Makes It Fundamentally Unfit for Democratic Society](https://saqibsafdar.com/the-mathematics-of-injustice-why-facial-recognitions-algorithmic-bias-makes-it-fundamentally-unfit-for-democratic-society/)

An argument about unequal error burdens, institutional power and the ethical implications of automated identification.

## Portfolio scope

This repository is an index to my published essays, with their full arguments and references available on my website. It is not a software implementation or a copy of the marked module submission. The essays present ethical analysis and arguments; they should not be read as experiments establishing causal learning effects.
